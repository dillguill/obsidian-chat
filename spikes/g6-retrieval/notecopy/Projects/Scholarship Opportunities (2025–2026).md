## Undergraduate Scholarships for African American Students

### UNCF Scholarships
 * **Deadline:** Varies (Rolling deadlines).
 * **Award Range:** $500 to $10,000+.
 * **Description:** UNCF manages a broad portfolio of scholarships specifically for Black students at all education levels.
### Ron Brown Scholar Program
 * **Deadline:** January 9 (Early), January 23 (Regular), 2026.
 * **Award:** $40,000 distributed over 4 years.
 * **Eligibility:** African American high school seniors demonstrating leadership and financial need.
### Thurgood Marshall College Fund Scholarships
 * **Deadline:** Varies by specific program.
 * **Award Range:** $500 to $10,000+.
 * **Eligibility:** Students must be attending public Historically Black Colleges and Universities (HBCUs) or Predominantly Black Institutions (PBIs).
### The Gates Scholarship
 * **Deadline:** September 15, 2025.
 * **Award:** Full cost of attendance.
 * **Eligibility:** Pell-eligible minority students, including African Americans.
### Jackie Robinson Foundation Scholarship
 * **Deadline:** January 10, 2026.
 * **Award:** Up to $35,000 over 4 years.
 * **Eligibility:** Black students with academic excellence, leadership, and community service. Includes a comprehensive scholars program with extra resources.
### Coca-Cola Scholars Program
 * **Deadline:** October 31, 2025.
 * **Award:** $20,000 to 150 students.
 * **Eligibility:** Merit-based; many recipients are African American leaders.
### Boule Foundation Scholarship
 * **Deadline:** March 2026.
 * **Award:** $4,000.
 * **Eligibility:** African American high school seniors with academic excellence.
### NANBPWC Scholarships
 * **Deadline:** March 2026.
 * **Award:** $1,000 to $3,000.
 * **Eligibility:** African American female high school seniors with community involvement.
### Regions Riding Forward Scholarship
 * **Deadline:** March 2026.
 * **Award:** $3,500 to $5,000.
 * **Eligibility:** Black high school seniors who write an essay about inspirational Black figures.
### National Black MBA Association Scholarships
 * **Deadline:** Varies by program.
 * **Award Range:** $1,000 to $10,000.
 * **Eligibility:** African American students pursuing business and leadership degrees.
## Graduate & Specialized Scholarships
### Laura Bassi Scholarship
 * **Deadline:** July 31, 2025.
 * **Award:** $500 to $2,500.
 * **Eligibility:** Master's and doctoral candidates.
### QuestBridge Graduate School Match (MBA)
 * **Deadline:** August 25, 2025.
 * **Award:** Full tuition.
 * **Eligibility:** Specifically for QuestBridge alumni.
### Zuckerman Postdoctoral Scholars Program
 * **Deadline:** August 2025 (TBA).
 * **Award:** $63,000.
 * **Eligibility:** Recent PhD graduates.
### AAUW International Fellowship
 * **Deadline:** November 15, 2025.
 * **Award:** Up to $30,000.
 * **Eligibility:** Women pursuing full-time graduate study abroad.
### NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program (GRFP)
 * **Deadline:** October 20, 2025.
 * **Award:** Three years of stipend and tuition.
 * **Eligibility:** STEM graduate students.
### Fulbright U.S. Student Program
 * **Deadline:** October 15, 2025.
 * **Award:** Funding for study or research.
 * **Eligibility:** Graduating seniors and graduate students.
### Churchill Scholarship
 * **Deadline:** October 10, 2025.
 * **Award:** Full tuition + £18,500.
 * **Eligibility:** U.S. STEM students for Cambridge University.
### Knight-Hennessy Scholars
 * **Deadline:** September 9, 2025.
 * **Award:** Tuition + stipend.
 * **Eligibility:** Graduate applicants to Stanford University for study/research.
### Microsoft Research PhD Fellowship
 * **Deadline:** November 2025 (TBA).
 * **Award:** $42,000 over 2 years.
 * **Eligibility:** Second-year PhD students in computing fields.
### Schlumberger Foundation Faculty for the Future
 * **Deadline:** November 10, 2025.
 * **Award:** Up to $50,000.
 * **Eligibility:** Women from developing countries for PhD/Master’s programs.
## Scholarships for Student Debt Reduction
### Charles Cheesman’s Student Debt Reduction Scholarship
 * **Deadline:** November 30, 2025.
 * **Award:** $10,240.
 * **Eligibility:** College and grad students who are African American or Hispanic/Latino, often first-generation students with student loan debt.
### A Heroes Family Scholarship
 * **Deadline:** March 30, 2026.
 * **Award:** $1,000.
 * **Eligibility:** High school seniors or above who have lost a parent/guardian in the line of duty (Law enforcement, fire, or military).
### Carlos F. Garcia Muentes Scholarship
 * **Deadline:** August 27, 2025.
 * **Award:** $500.
 * **Eligibility:** First or second-generation immigrants and first-generation college students.
