## The Core Concept: Acquiring "Boomer" Businesses
Instead of starting a company from scratch with a new idea, this strategy focuses on **business acquisition**. You identify established businesses owned by individuals ready to retire (often referred to as "Baby Boomers").
### Key Advantages
 * **Proven Model:** The business is already operational and generating revenue.
 * **Existing Infrastructure:** It comes with a built-in customer base, trained employees, and established processes.
 * **Immediate Cash Flow:** In the example provided, the acquisition produces approximately **$30,000 in monthly revenue**.
## The Acquisition Process
Buying a business does not necessarily require having the full purchase price in cash upfront. Most deals are structured through various financing layers.
### Financing the Deal
 * **SBA Financing:** Small Business Administration loans can cover up to **90%** of the acquisition cost.
 * **Seller Financing:** The current owner may agree to finance a portion of the sale, allowing the buyer to pay them back over time from the business's profits.
 * **Lower Personal Capital:** Because of these financing options, the buyer only needs to provide a small percentage of the total price.
### Essential Steps (Due Diligence)
Before finalizing any purchase, a buyer must:
 1. **Review Financials:** Verify the tax returns, profit and loss statements, and cash flow.
 2. **Interview the Owner:** Understand the daily operations and the reason for the sale.
 3. **Evaluate the Numbers:** Ensure the revenue is sustainable and can cover the debt service of the loans.
