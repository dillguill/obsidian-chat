Our Medium Conditioner is geared towards both & hair textures looking for hydration, slippage & curl definition. Your curls will dance to their own unique rhythm after conditioning. Featuring a rich blend of Jojoba Oil & Olive Oil as key ingredients, our Medium Conditioner with slip is great for keeping low to normal porosity curls nourished & defined yet not weighed down.
