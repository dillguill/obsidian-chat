I recently got back from my trip visiting Megan in Winston-Salem. I feel better about where we are in our relationship since I got to see her. I was worried that being apart for so long would affect things but I’m glad to say I still love her and feel that attraction. I think sometimes where we’ll be in the future and how we’ll move forward. I suggested moving in together at some point because I want to see if we’re truly compatible in that regard. 

Aside from that when I got back it was kind of bitter. My dads changing our phone plan to something called Flash mobile to get some extra points so he can get $3000 from ACN. He didn’t consult us or research it he just decided as usual. I’m exhausted with him dictating this house and making decisions in his own interest and passing it off as him thinking about us. Yes, he provides but it’s always the bare minimum. It feels like he saves the extravagant stuff for other people and leaves us with the leftovers. Whether it’s the fire station cooking, heart-to-heart talks with other peoples sons, or gifts to clients or employees. I though being the ideal son would get me grace and it always bothered me how Dawson was treated like the prodigal son. But now I kind of feel neither of us are valued as the men we are and his attention and kindness are conditional, as they’ve always been. It feels like the only way to get him to help is to do what he wants you to do. It frustrates me to no end with the fact that no one sees the problem with this because “it’s just the way things are.” Dawson and I have worked our asses off our whole lives and now it feels like the one time we want to do something that we want to do that we feel will lead to greater happiness and success no one wants to support it. I get how an online business is untraditional and I’ve had my doubts, but with the work and talent that me and Dawson apply ourselves with I just wish that we had the confidence and support of the people around us. It feels like I’m going back into my basketball days mindset of having “haters” which I know isn’t the case but it’s hard not to feel resentment when the people around you only seem to want to tell you you should quit.
