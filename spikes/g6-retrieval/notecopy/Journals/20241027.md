[[Victoria Vignaud|Victoria]] announced her pregnancy today…the news left me feeling numb. I guess some part of me was still holding on to some type of hope or just waiting for something. Honestly, it feels like my life has passed me by, and I have nothing to show for it. It made me think that when you are caught up in indecision, life decides for you, and often times you have to just deal with the hand that you are left with. I think this represents an ending to a chapter of a book that was never written. There will always be a part of me that loves her, that wonders what if, but now there’s no more what if or what could’ve been, and I’ll have to focus my energy on moving forward and discovering and crafting the life that I have left in front of me.
