I'm kind of upset right now with [[Victoria Vignaud|Victoria]] I haven't talked to her in like a day because she left the state without telling me when she'd promised that she'd hang out with me to celebrate my birthday on Saturday. I'm over it a little and she sort of apologized admitting she ditched me and halfheartedly apologized but I'm more upset at the fact that she does this all the time, standing me up, for last minute events or random friends popping into town. I guess it's just because I feel like I drop so much and dedicate so much time to help her and it feels like she doesn't value me for who I am but what I can do for her which is basically free rides, entertainment when she's bored, homework and free grades, and walking her roommates dog.

She brought up I think a couple of weeks ago that we'd known each other for like a year and a half. It struck me because hardly any of my friends stick around that long or I just end up disappearing. It was surreal to think about all the time and history I have with this person but it's also partially saddening to think she probably doesn't value the time as much as I do.

I know it sounds whiny or needy but just once sometimes I wish I could feel appreciated for the times I go out of my way for others. The blood, sweat, and tears I wrench from my flesh and bone to make those around me happy and better, it'd make the world for me to just hear a thank you. A "thank you Dillon, for all that you do for me, thank you for being a ray of light that brings brightness and joy to my day and for being their for me no matter what."
